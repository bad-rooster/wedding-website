# 🌄 **2-Day Travel Itinerary: London → Llandeilo**

---

# **DAY 1 — Travel + Explore Llandeilo Town**

## 🚆 Morning: Travel from London to Llandeilo

**Recommended route (fastest & easiest):**

- **08:48** – Depart **London Paddington**
- **10:54** – Arrive **Swansea**
- **11:22** – Depart Swansea → Llandeilo (Heart of Wales Line)
- **12:10** – Arrive **Llandeilo**

(You can replace this with a later time if you prefer — there are trains roughly every hour to Swansea and several daily connections onwards.)

**Alternative:** Drive (≈ 3.5–4 hours).
Depart London at **8am**, arrive **12pm**.

## 🥗 Lunch (12:30–14:00)

**Options in town:**

- **The Cawdor Restaurant** – modern Welsh food
- **Ginhaus Deli** – famous for its gin collection & artisan lunches
- **The Hangout** – casual café with excellent coffee

---

## 🌿 Afternoon: Explore Llandeilo (14:00–17:30)

### **1. Walk around the colourful town streets**

Boutiques, galleries, Welsh craft shops.

### **2. Visit Dinefwr Estate (National Trust)**

- Newton House (18th-century mansion)
- Deer park
- Hill walk to Dinefwr Castle ruins (stunning views of the Towy Valley)

It’s one of the top attractions and easy to reach from town.

---

## 🍽️ Dinner (18:30–20:00)

**Top recommendations:**

- **The Plough Rhosmaen Restaurant** – excellent Welsh produce
- **The Cawdor** – imaginative seasonal menu
- **Foxy’s Steakhouse** (Slightly outside town)

---

## 🌙 Optional Evening Activity (20:00–22:00)

- Scenic stroll over **Llandeilo Bridge** at sunset
- Quiet pub stops: **The White Horse**, **The Angel**

Rest at your hotel.

---

# **DAY 2 — Countryside Walks + Castles + Gardens**

## ☕ Breakfast (08:00)

Enjoy breakfast at your accommodation — most do excellent Welsh breakfasts.

---

## 🏞️ Morning Activity (09:00–12:00)

### **Option A — Carreg Cennen Castle**

A dramatic clifftop castle 15 minutes from town.
One of Wales’s most spectacular medieval fortresses.

### **Option B — Brecon Beacons National Park (Black Mountain area)**

Short walks or scenic drives with panoramic views.

---

## 🍽️ Lunch (12:30–13:30)

- **Carreg Cennen Tearoom** (if visiting the castle)
- **Cawdor Bar** back in Llandeilo
- **Pitchfork & Provision** – casual but high-quality food

---

## 🌼 Afternoon Activity (13:45–16:30)

### **National Botanic Garden of Wales (15 minutes away)**

- Massive glasshouse
- Tropical gardens
- Butterfly house
- Rare plants & Welsh flora trails

A relaxed, peaceful way to end your trip.

---

## 🍰 Snack / Coffee (16:30–17:00)

Stop at the garden café or return to **Ginhaus** in Llandeilo for cake/coffee.

---

## 🚆 Return to London (17:00–21:30)

Example:

- **17:23** Llandeilo → Swansea
- **18:55** Swansea → London Paddington
- **21:15** Arrive London

(You can choose an earlier or later service depending on preference.)
