Here are some accommodation options near the venue in Carmarthenshire, Wales.

## Recommended Places to Stay

- **[Cawdor Hotel](https://www.thecawdor.com/)**  
  A historic Georgian building in the heart of Llandeilo, offering boutique-style rooms and a warm atmosphere. Ideal if you want to stay centrally.

- **[The White Horse](http://www.visitllandeilo.co.uk/food-and-drink/the-white-horse-inn/)**  
  A welcoming pub with rooms, offering comfortable accommodation with a relaxed, local feel.

- **[Emlyn Arms, Llanarthne](https://www.booking.com/hotel/gb/emlyn-arms.en-gb.html)**  
  A traditional Welsh inn right next to the venue, great for those who want to be nearby without venturing far.

## Other Options

There are also plenty of holiday cottages, farmhouses, and private rooms available through **Airbnb** in the surrounding Carmarthenshire area. Search for properties near Llandeilo or Llanarthne for the best proximity to the venue.
